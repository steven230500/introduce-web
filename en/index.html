On screen

A Sunday at the desk

From putting the service together the night before to switching the projector off. The app runs in Spanish or English; the screenshots below show it in Spanish.

Saturday9:40pm

The service comes together in one list

Songs, passages, videos, pictures and the points of the sermon, in the order of the service. Drag to reorder, and duplicate last Sunday to start the next one.

  • Songs from ProPresenter, OpenLP, SongSelect or ChordPro come in after you review them one by one. Duplicates stay out.
  • The design editor warns you when the words will not read from the back row, and a long psalm shrinks to fit instead of running off the bottom.
  • Everyone on the church's team shares the songs, designs and colours.

Sunday7:50am

Plug in the projector, and that is it

Choose once which screen it projects to, and the app remembers. If the building's internet is down, the service starts anyway: everything prepared is already on the computer.

  • While people arrive, the waiting screen shows the church's name and a countdown. W puts it up and takes it down.
  • Nine moving backgrounds come built in. They weigh nothing and need no internet.

Worship10:00am

Nobody sees the wrong slide

The cursor moves through the list without touching what the congregation sees. What comes next is in view, and it goes up when the band reaches the chorus.

  • The musicians get their own stage display: what is up, what is next, and messages from the desk.
  • Any phone on the same Wi-Fi can change slides. It connects with a QR code and a PIN, with nothing to install.
  • For the livestream, a window with just the lyrics over green or black, ready for OBS.

Sermon11:15am

The sermon runs long, and the team knows

A verse that was not in the list is four keys away: V, “jn 3:16”, Enter. The Bible built in is the Reina-Valera 1960, the Spanish text, complete and offline.

  • An announcement appears over the slide and clears itself: “Children to the classroom.”
  • A message only the preacher sees, on the stage display: “Five minutes left.”

4:42 / 4:00 +0:42 Each item counts its time against the rehearsal, at the desk and on the stage display.

Afterwards12:40pm

The service ends, the report is ready

Everything projected is logged with each song's CCLI number, for the licence report. What ran during rehearsal does not count. Next Sunday starts by duplicating this one.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Unzip the file and drag Introduce to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify it: click Done.
  3. In System Settings, Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway.

Only the first time. The app is not signed with an Apple certificate yet.
