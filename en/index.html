On screen

Free church presentation software for lyrics, Bible verses, announcements and video, run from a single window. Built for whoever is at the desk on Sunday, even if they learned it two weeks ago.

Try the screen above: ← and → change slides, B turns it black. They are the same keys as in the app.

Saturday9:40pm

The service comes together in one list

Songs, passages, videos, pictures, announcements and the points of the sermon, in the order of the service. Drag to reorder, and duplicate last Sunday to start the next one.

Songs from another program
ProPresenter 4 to 7, FreeShow, OpenLP, SongSelect, ChordPro or plain text. You review them one by one with the lyrics alongside, and duplicates stay out.
A new song
Paste lyrics copied from anywhere and the app suggests where each slide should break.
PDF and PowerPoint
Announcements in PDF come in as one presentation with their pages, exactly as they were designed, with nothing to install. A PowerPoint does the same if the computer has LibreOffice, or comes in as editable text.
Moments of the service
Worship, Sermon, Announcements: a coloured mark divides the list, what is done folds away, and a whole part moves with its songs.
The sermon, point by point
A title and its points, each on its own slide. Corrected when the preacher changes their mind, without typing them again.
Find anything with ⌘K
Songs, passages, designs and actions from one place.
Photos and videos
The church's library, showing how much room is left before you upload.
No internet, no problem
Create, copy and delete services offline. The changes go out by themselves, in order, when the network is back.
The whole team

Saturday10:30pm

Designs that read from the back row

A solid colour, a gradient, a photo or a looping video. Typeface, size, line spacing, margins, shadow and transition. A simple mode to go fast, and a layers mode with a canvas, guides and snapping to place every block by hand.

Real contrast
The editor warns you when the words will not read, measuring the photo behind them too.
Text that fits
A long psalm shrinks to fit instead of running off the bottom edge.
Lift the words
When heads block the bottom of the screen, the design lifts every line at once - with ⌥ and the arrows, even mid-service.
Colours from the photo
The photo offers its own palette, and the church keeps its colours for every design.
Undo
⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z, and the app asks before closing with unsaved changes.
Moving backgrounds
Nine scenes built in that weigh nothing and need no internet, plus the church's own pictures and videos.
Videos without internet
The first Sunday a video is used it is kept on the computer, and plays from there with no network.

Sunday7:50am

Plug everything in, and that is it

Choose once which screen it projects to, and the app remembers. If the church's internet is down, the service starts anyway: everything prepared is already on the computer.

Waiting screen
An animated scene with the church's name, a message and the time or a countdown. W puts it up and takes it down.
Stage display
A separate window for the preacher and the musicians: what is up, what is next, and messages from the desk.
A phone as a remote
Any phone on the same Wi-Fi, with no internet and nothing to install: scan a QR code and type the PIN. It is remembered for next Sunday.
Closed until you open it
Phone control is off until someone turns it on. Changing the PIN disconnects every phone.
A window for the livestream
Just the lyrics over green, blue, magenta or black, at the top or the bottom, on a bar or outlined. Capture it in OBS with a Chroma Key.
In Spanish or English
It asks the first time. The projector and the stage display speak the same language as the desk.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Unzip the file and drag Introduce to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify it: click Done.
  3. In System Settings, Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway.

Only the first time. The app is not signed with an Apple certificate yet. When a new version is out, the app says so.

Installing on Windows

  1. Unzip the file and open the Introduce folder.
  2. Run Introduce.exe.
  3. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Questions

How much does Introduce cost?

It is free to start. Download it without paying, create your church's account, and no card is asked for.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The service starts and projects with no network: the Bible, the designs, the backgrounds and the videos already used are on the computer. Anything prepared offline goes out by itself when the connection is back. You need a connection to create the account and share with the team.

Which Bible does it include?

It comes with the complete Reina-Valera 1909, the Spanish text in the public domain. The version your church uses (RVR1960, NVI, LBLA or another) is imported once from a Zefania, OSIS, OpenSong, Beblia or FreeShow file and stays on the computer. Those translations belong to their publishers, so Introduce does not hand them out: each church uses its own. All of them work offline; browse by book or type the reference, such as “jn 3:16” or “sal 23”.

What computer do I need?

A Mac with macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. For Windows 10 or 11 there is a beta version; Linux comes after.

Does it work for livestreaming?

Yes. It opens a window with just the lyrics over green, blue, magenta or black, which you capture in OBS and key over the camera with a Chroma Key or Luma Key filter.

Can I use two screens?

Yes. The projector goes to the screen you choose and the app remembers it. The stage display opens in another window, for the preacher and the musicians.

Can I change slides from my phone?

Yes, from any phone on the same Wi-Fi, with no internet and nothing to install. Turn it on in the app, scan the QR code and type the PIN.

Can several people at the church use it?

Yes. People ask to join the church and an administrator approves them. A church can have several administrators, and if someone forgets their password, an administrator gives them a code to set a new one. Songs, designs and colours are shared.

Is it in English?

Yes, in Spanish or English. It asks for the language the first time, and you can change it later in Settings.

Built in a church, for churches.

Everything it does exists because something went wrong on a Sunday. If your team is missing something, ask for it.
