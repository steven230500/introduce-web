The screen at the front,

What it solves

The whole service in one list

Songs, passages, video, images and the points of the sermon in a single order. Drag to rearrange, rename anything in place, and duplicate last Sunday to build the next one in a minute.

  • The cursor moves without touching what the room is looking at.
  • It goes to the screen when you send it, not before.
  • The Up next panel shows what the next press will do.

The Bible is inside, and it works offline

A complete Bible ships with the app, no connection required. Browse by book, or type the reference straight in and the passage arrives already split into slides that fit.

  • Works with the router switched off.
  • A last-minute verse is four keystrokes.
  • Reina-Valera 1960 today. Public-domain English translations are the next thing being added.

Designs that read from the back row

Solid, gradient or photo backgrounds. Typeface, size, margins, transition. A simple mode to move fast, and a layers mode with a canvas, guides and snapping when someone wants every block placed by hand.

  • A long psalm shrinks to fit instead of being cut off.
  • The app warns you when text will not be readable, with the real contrast ratio.
  • Colours from the background photo are offered as a palette.
  • Undo and redo, and a warning before you close on unsaved work.

Announcements in the middle of the sermon

One line over the slide for the whole room, with a clock so it takes itself down. Or a message only the platform sees on the stage display. The ones you show every week are saved and one press away.

  • «Children are dismissed» without typing it again.
  • «Five minutes left» without anyone else reading it.

Stage display

A separate window for the preacher and the musicians: what is on screen, what is next, and messages from the desk.

No internet

The service starts and runs with the network down. Edits are held and sent by themselves when it comes back.

More than one screen

You choose which one it projects to, and it remembers. Nobody crawls behind the rack during the first song.

Your team

Several people in the same church, sharing the songs, the designs and the media library.
